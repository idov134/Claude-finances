  • Published anomalies decay after publication; a 50% haircut is applied but the true decay is unknown.
  • Costs are modeled, not observed; paper trading must confirm them.
  • LLM news signals cannot be backtested before the model's knowledge cutoff.
  • See docs/PLAN.md for what cannot be verified with the available data.
